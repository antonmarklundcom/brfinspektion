near-zero direct search volume — confirms brfentreprenad.se has no organic
traffic value of its own; it functions only as a brand/redirect domain.

**Read on this dataset:** low absolute volume across the board — this
confirms the business is a low-volume/high-value-per-lead niche, not a
volume-SEO play. CPC ranges up to 136 SEK on "besiktning brf" and 81 SEK on
"5 års besiktning brf" show real commercial intent and real competition —
size Google Ads budget expecting cost-per-click in the tens-to-hundreds of
SEK range, and expect a small number of monthly searches nationally, so
national (not city-by-city) targeting is correct.

**Two service verticals this data reveals beyond the core stambyte focus:**

1. **OVK-besiktning** — statutory ventilation inspection, recurring every
   3–6 years. Highest single volume in this dataset (70/mo on the primary
   term). Recurring-revenue service line, likely already covered by Partner
   A's certifications. Build as its own service page.

2. **Garantibesiktning (2-year / 5-year)** — statutory post-construction
   inspections under AB04/ABT06. This is Partner A's core competence and,
   critically, **every BRF that completes a stambyte through this funnel is
   an automatic future customer** for both the 2-year and 5-year
   garantibesiktning — model this as a CRM/follow-up trigger, not just an SEO
   page. Consolidate the 2yr/5yr/bostadsrätt/lägenhet/brf phrasing variants
   into one well-structured page with FAQPage schema rather than building
   separate thin pages per variant.
